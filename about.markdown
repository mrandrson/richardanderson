My name is Richard Anderson, I'm 19 years old, and I am studying physics and mathematics at the University of Texas at El Paso. I started working on a project in theoretical astrophysics at the beginning of my senior year in Burges High School, and have been working with the same professor ([Dr. Kedron Silsbee]()) since then. During my time in undergrad, I have worked on applications of deep learning to computer vision at the UTEP Aerospace Center. I have also recently began working in the Neutrino and Rare Event Searches Group at the University of Texas at Arlington, specifically I have worked on data analysis of the NEXT experiment, as well as effective field theory studies using the Python nuDOBE package. 

In the future, it is my hope that I will have the opportunity to work on projects in High Energy Theory. Specifically, I hope to work on problems in conformal field theory.
